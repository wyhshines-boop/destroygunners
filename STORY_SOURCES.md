# Story background sources

The Destroy Gunners ZZ section uses the original Japanese and English game text extracted in `tools/dgzz_tutorial125/strings.json` in the GREE workspace:

- String 996: opening story — wars on a planet at the edge of the galaxy; scientist and demon program; activation after an attack on the master server; infected Destroyers; survivors underground; formation of Destroy Gunners years later.
- String 997: first-mission introduction — advance into Madlum, objective to eliminate the Destroyers and destroy the master server, enemy interception force waiting.

Japanese establishes the planetary location and completion of counter-Destroyer mechs. The website summarizes these scenes without adding factions, dates, mercenary identities, or a shared setting for the entire series. Chinese, English and Japanese copy convey the same background. The earlier unsourced archivist commentary was removed. This revision does not verify or rewrite the separate Assault Gunners section.
